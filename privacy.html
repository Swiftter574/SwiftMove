Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

SwiftMove ("we," "us," "our") provides moving and home services and operates this website and related customer communications. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through our website, booking forms, customer conversations, and connected service platforms.

1. Information we receive

  • Contact and scheduling details: your name, telephone number, email, job address, preferred dates, availability and access information.
  • Job details: requested services, item descriptions, photographs you choose to provide, measurements, project scope, estimates, schedule changes and service records.
  • Communications: messages sent through our website, Thumbtack or other services; and relevant notes about appointments and customer requests.
  • Website and technical information: limited device/browser or usage information that our hosting and service providers may process when you visit the site.
  • Payment-related records: amounts billed, paid, or outstanding when we invoice you; payment processors, when used, may handle your payment method information under their own privacy notices.

2. How we use information

We use relevant information to respond to questions, evaluate job scope and safety, provide estimates, arrange and perform services, coordinate technicians, handle approved changes to work, follow up about a requested service, keep business records, troubleshoot issues, prevent duplicate or fraudulent requests, and comply with applicable law.

3. Automated assistants and customer messages

SwiftMove may use software and AI-assisted tools to classify service inquiries, prepare estimates, draft or send routine customer-service messages where the relevant platform permits, coordinate scheduling, and notify our team. Automated estimates and scheduling messages may require human review, and a requested appointment is not confirmed until SwiftMove expressly confirms it. If you want a person to review an automated response, contact us using the email below.

We do not treat a service inquiry alone as permission to send unrelated promotional text messages. Any marketing messages will follow applicable consent and opt-out requirements. You can ask us to stop nonessential promotional contact.

4. Service providers and other parties

We may use service providers to operate the website and process business communications, scheduling, automation, AI-assisted analysis and customer records, such as GitHub Pages, Google services, n8n, Telegram and, when connected and authorized, third-party lead platforms including Thumbtack. Providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy practices. We share only information reasonably needed to operate these services, provide your requested job, or comply with law. We do not sell customer personal information.

5. Retention and security

We keep business and customer information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage requests, complete services, maintain records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations and protect our business. Retention periods depend on the information and purpose. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online system is completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive payment-card or government-identification information through ordinary messages.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You may email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information SwiftMove maintains about you, or to ask questions about communications. We will review requests and respond as required by applicable law, subject to legal and operational exceptions. Requests about data independently held by another platform may also need to be directed to that platform.

7. Third-party platforms and links

When you communicate with us through Thumbtack or another third-party service, that service's terms and privacy policy also apply to its independent handling of your data. Our website may link to third-party forms, maps or booking tools that have their own privacy practices.

8. Children

Our services and online inquiries are intended for adults making home-service arrangements. We do not knowingly solicit personal information directly from children.

9. Updates

We may update this policy when our services, tools or legal requirements change. The effective date above shows when this version took effect.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact swiftmovexeme@gmail.com.
